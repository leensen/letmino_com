Letmino Mileage

Now on Google Play

Mileage logging with one photo of your odometer

No more reconstructing trips afterwards. You photograph your odometer, Letmino Mileage reads the reading, recognises where you are via GPS and closes the trip automatically. At the end of the month your claim is ready as Excel.

  • No account
  • No cloud
  • No ads
  • English and Dutch

How it works

Two taps per trip. The app does the rest.

  1. 1 👆

    Tap the widget

    One tap on the widget or the big button and the camera opens right away.

  2. 2 📷

    Photograph your odometer

    The reading is read from the photo automatically (on-device text recognition) and pre-filled. One tap to correct.

  3. 3 📍

    GPS recognises the place

    Home, office or at a customer: known places are recognised and filled in as the address.

  4. 4 ✅

    Save

    The trip is closed automatically with distance, addresses and description. Done.

Everything for a complete mileage log

From the first photo to the annual report for your records.

🧠

Smart logging

  • Known places: set up home, office and regular customers once; the app recognises them from then on
  • Drove privately without logging? The app spots the gap and suggests "private", without tracking private trips
  • Forgot a reading? Enter it afterwards with the right date and time, or split a trip at a forgotten stop
📊

Claims and tax records

  • Month overview with live validation: non-increasing readings, trips without a description and unusual distances get a red flag before you export
  • Monthly claim as a clean Excel (or CSV) in a standard claim format, with your employer in the title
  • Rate per kilometre or mile with a start date: when the rate changes, every trip is calculated with the rate that applied on its date
  • Annual report with first and last reading, all trips (including private), all readings with photo as evidence and a completeness check
  • Mark a month as processed and free up space by deleting the photos of periods that are done
🌍

Kilometres or miles

  • Distance unit and currency are configurable; the defaults follow your phone's region
  • Available in English and Dutch
🔒

Your data stays yours

  • Everything is stored on your device: no account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking
  • Full backup as a zip (data + photos) that you keep yourself and restore on a new device

Frequently asked questions

Does the app track my location in the background?

No. GPS is only used at the moment you record an odometer reading, to recognise the place. The app does not track trips and does not run in the background.

Where are my data and photos stored?

Only on your own phone. There is no account and no cloud. With the backup function you create a zip with all data and photos yourself, which you restore on a new device.

Does the text recognition work without internet?

Yes. The odometer reading is read from the photo on your device. Only converting GPS coordinates into an address needs an internet connection.

Which files can I export?

Per month a claim as Excel or CSV in a standard claim format, and per year a report with all trips, all readings and the photos as evidence.

Can I add a forgotten trip afterwards?

Yes. You enter a reading afterwards with the right date and time, or split an existing trip at a forgotten stop. Miles driven privately are marked as private with one tap.

Is Letmino Mileage suitable for tax records?

The annual report contains first and last reading, all trips including private ones, all readings with photo as evidence and a completeness check. That gives you the substantiation a mileage log requires. Always check the current rules of your tax authority for your situation.
