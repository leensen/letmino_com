FishTrip

Fishing map with catch-chance heatmap and catch log

Available now

FishTrip is the ultimate fishing app for Dutch freshwater. The app combines live weather and water data, biological species knowledge and your own catches into a smart heatmap that shows exactly your chances of a catch.

  • Smart catch-chance heatmap
  • Catch-chance timeline up to 5 days ahead
  • 100% offline AI fishing expert
  • Log catches and calibrate the heatmap
