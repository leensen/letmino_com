About Letmino

"Let me know" — three words that say it all. We help you understand the world.

Our mission

The world is full of information, data and complexity. Letmino builds tools that translate this complexity into understanding. Not by making it simpler than it is, but by making it more accessible.

We believe technology, and AI in particular, is not meant to impress. It is meant to help. That is why we do not build "AI-first" products full of jargon and hype. We build practical tools that happen to use smart technology.

"No AI wow. Just: this helps."

Our values

✨

Simplicity

Complexity belongs in the technology, not in the interface. Our products are immediately understandable and usable.

🎯

Practical

Every feature must solve a concrete problem. We do not build for the technology, but for the result.

🤝

Honest

No hidden costs, no data harvesting, no misleading claims. What you see is what you get.
