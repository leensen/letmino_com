Frequently asked questions

When will the apps be released?

The first Study apps (Multiply and Divide) are currently in development. We expect to launch them in the Google Play Store in the first half of 2026.

Are the apps free?

This depends on the app. We use a fair model and may ask a small one-time payment for a full version. We do not use ads in our apps.

Will there be iOS apps?

Technically this is possible. It is not on the roadmap at the moment.

Can I have a custom app built?

No, we do not build custom apps on request.
